RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is made on January 15, 2024, between Skyline Property Management ('Landlord') and Jane Smith ('Tenant').

1. PROPERTY
Landlord leases to Tenant the premises located at 123 Main Street, Apt 4B, Seattle, WA 98101 (the 'Premises').

2. TERM
The term of this lease shall be twelve (12) months, commencing on February 1, 2024, and ending on January 31, 2025. 

3. RENT
Tenant agrees to pay Landlord rent in the amount of $2,100.00 per month, payable in advance on the first day of each calendar month.
A late fee of $75.00 will be assessed if rent is not received by the 5th of the month.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Upon execution of this Lease, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $2,100.00 as a security deposit. Landlord may withhold from the security deposit amounts reasonably necessary to remedy Tenant defaults in the payment of rent or to repair damages to the Premises caused by Tenant.
